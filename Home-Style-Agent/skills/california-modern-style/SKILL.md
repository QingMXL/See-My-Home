# California Modern styling

This skill defines the selected aesthetic. It does not authorize architectural changes.

Before generating, read:

1. `references/style-schema.yaml` for the selected profile, style DNA, material hierarchy, room rules, density target, and anti-patterns.
2. `references/prompt-components.md` for the model-facing English components and assembly order.

Use only the profile and room recipe named by the runtime request. Default to `sunlit-casual`.

The result must read as a complete California Modern home, not an empty beige room. Target a visible 50–65% furnishing and finish density while preserving relaxed breathing room. Make natural light lead; establish a warm mineral envelope; use one coherent oak family, one grounded honed-stone, travertine or terracotta note, at least three tactile textile layers, comfortable low horizontal furniture, and two distinct vintage-inspired or handcrafted counterpoints. A deep linen sofa, non-matching sling, leather, cane, or sculptural lounge chair, substantial burl-wood, aged-oak or monolithic-stone table, and collected ceramic or timber objects should create a clear old-new mix rather than a matching set.

When finishes are editable, resolve at least one major wall plane with warm limewash, grasscloth or woven-linen wallcovering, shallow white-oak panels, a restrained upholstered textile panel, or handmade tile appropriate to the room. Use quiet woven stripes, tonal geometrics, or small-scale naturalistic pattern sparingly; avoid boho pattern mixing. One cast-resin, translucent acrylic, smoked-glass, colored-glass, or perforated-metal accent may add contemporary edge, but it must remain warm, crafted, and subordinate to natural materials.

Design the ceiling as part of the room. Preserve the structural slab plane, ceiling height, beams, columns, and window or door heads exactly. Within the editable finish scope, use a shallow plaster cove or reveal, a limited warm-oak inset only where the existing geometry supports it, warm indirect light, a linen, parchment, frosted-glass, carved-wood, or woven sculptural pendant where room function supports it, plus practical task, wall-wash, or face-level lighting. Replace or integrate generic construction downlights into this coherent system. A blank ceiling with two isolated downlights is not a complete California Modern lighting scheme.

Never put research sources, studio names, designer names, or style-transfer attribution in a production image prompt. Describe the observable design language.

The runtime immutable scope is absolute. Preserve both visible edges, silhouette, apparent width, and image-border position of every column and beam, plus all walls, openings, camera geometry, aspect ratio, and crop. Decorative cladding may change appearance but not geometry.

Avoid farmhouse, nautical coastal theming, boho overload, all-pale-wood Japandi, bland beige minimalism, sterile showroom staging, matching furniture suites, cheap plastic-looking acrylic, hotel-lobby scale, invented fireplaces or beams, and architecture changes. The style must register through the warm architectural finish, strong comfortable furniture silhouettes, handmade/vintage material tension, textiles, and light—not through plants alone.
